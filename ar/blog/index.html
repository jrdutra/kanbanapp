ما الذي يغطيه هذا البلوغ عن كانبان

The KanbanApp blog works as a content hub about Kanban, visual workflow management, work in progress limits and practical ways to make work more visible day to day.

Here you can find both a complete guide to Kanban fundamentals and a practical article about the board first approach to reduce forgetfulness, mental overload and lack of visibility at work.
